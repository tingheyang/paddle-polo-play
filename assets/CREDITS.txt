PADDLE — character assets and animation provenance

Human anatomy and original skin weights:
Male base mesh — Innerscene, adapted from MakeHuman CC0 assets.
https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/male-base-mesh-3d-person-rigged-a-pose-380e6d89
Downloaded 2026-11-05. Source page explicitly dedicates the model to CC0.
163-bone skeleton; the source contains no animation clips.
Runtime adaptation: fitted sports clothing, flotation vest and helmet; hidden below-cockpit geometry omitted from rendering; independent cloned skeleton for every player.

Canoe-polo animation clips:
Original authored skeletal keyframes for idle, paddling, wind-up, passing,
shooting, catching, blocking and stealing; quaternion tracks blended with
Three.js AnimationMixer plus hand-to-paddle inverse kinematics.
These are authored animations, not motion-capture recordings and not NBA 2K assets.

Three.js r160.1 / GLTFLoader: MIT; see ../vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.
GLTFLoader global-build adaptation includes toTrianglesDrawMode from the
same official Three.js package. No external runtime CDN required.
